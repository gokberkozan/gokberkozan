### 📁 Featured Projects

#### 🎬 [The Movies – Data Analysis](https://github.com/gokberkozan/the-movies-analysis)
What makes a film successful? End-to-end analysis of 45K+ TMDB films covering budget, revenue, genre, runtime and ratings.
- Found a ~73% correlation between log budget and log revenue, yet ~30% of films lost money
- Compared 5 regression models to predict box office revenue; best model reached **R² = 0.523**
- `BigQuery` `dbt` `Python` `scikit-learn` `Power BI`

#### 💼 [LinkedIn Job Market Analytics](https://github.com/gokberkozan/LinkedIn-job-postings-analysis)
Analysis of **124K U.S. LinkedIn job postings (2023–2024)** across industries, locations, company size and engagement.
- Drill-down from industry → company → position, geographic demand mapping and statistical hypothesis testing
- Salary prediction with Linear Regression (IQR outlier handling, one-hot encoding)
- Interactive Power BI dashboard · [dbt models](https://github.com/gokberkozan/linkedin-job-postings)
- `BigQuery` `dbt` `SQL` `Python` `scikit-learn` `Power BI`

#### 🗄️ [SQL Practice](https://github.com/gokberkozan/SQL)
12 hands-on exercise sets on the dvdrental sample database: filtering, aggregation, GROUP BY / HAVING, JOINs, set operations (UNION / INTERSECT / EXCEPT) and subqueries.
- `PostgreSQL`
